The Socials Committee of the Society of Physics Students (SPS) is dedicated to building a strong, vibrant community by organizing social events and activities that foster camaraderie and collaboration among students. Our goal is to create fun, inclusive opportunities for members to connect outside the classroom, share their passion for physics, and engage in meaningful discussions.
From arts and crafts and movie screenings to picnics and hiking trips, the Socials Committee ensures that there’s something for everyone. We strive to make the SPS a welcoming space where all students can relax, bond, and feel part of a supportive network. Whether you’re looking to make new friends or unwind with fellow physics enthusiasts, we’ve got something for you! Reach out to Nadia Sun ([nadiasun@berkeley.edu](mailto:nadiasun@berkeley.edu)) if you'd like to join!

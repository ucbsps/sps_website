The projects committee works on implementing new ideas to better service the
physics community. Projects can range dramatically in scope: from working with
local educational programs in the name of science communication to designing new
stickers for physics majors! The projects committee currently meets bi-weekly on
Wednesdays from 3-4pm. To learn more about the committee, feel free to message
Ayush Guha ([ayushguha@berkeley.edu](mailto:ayushguha@berkeley.edu)) or Meghna Subramanium ([meghna.sub@berkeley.edu](mailto:meghna.sub@berkeley.edu))!

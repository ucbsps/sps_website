### What We Do

We host a variety of events to help bring the physics community at Berkeley closer! Members can pursue their interests in physics in rigorous competitions or more laidback socials; SPS is whatever you make of it. We are also here to help support you, so please feel free to talk to other members and officers about how to make the most of your physics experience at Cal!
